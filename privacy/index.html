Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MicaLingo ("we", "us", or "our") processes personal data when you use our website at micalingo.com and our mobile application (together, the "Service"). We process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data-protection laws. Operator details are published in our Impressum.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data in connection with the Service is MicaLingo, operator of https://micalingo.com and the MicaLingo mobile application. For privacy-related requests, contact us at support.micalingo@gmail.com. Further operator information is available in the Impressum.

2. Information We Process

Sign-in is provided exclusively through Google Sign-In (Google Authentication). When you choose to sign in, Google may share with us a limited set of account information (such as your Google account identifier, display name, profile photo URL, and email address) as permitted by Google’s authentication APIs and your Google account settings. We do not operate an independent password-based account system, and we do not have visibility into your Google password or the full contents of your Google account. We may also store learning progress, imported vocabulary, and similar content that you voluntarily create or upload within the Service, linked to your authenticated account.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Service uses cookies and similar storage technologies (for example localStorage) on the website and in the mobile application.

Necessary cookies and local storage: we store strictly necessary information so the Service can function, including your language preference, cookie-consent choice, and session or login state. These are required to provide the Service you request and do not require consent.

Advertising cookies (Google): a third party — Google — uses cookies to serve advertisements through Google AdSense (and related Google advertising services). These cookies may be used to show ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites, and may collect data such as IP address, device and browser information, and how you interact with ads.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): if you consent, Google Analytics measures how the website and the app are used. This includes which pages are opened, when a quiz is started or finished, when the Android app file is downloaded, the first time the installed Android app is opened, and when the support button is clicked. It is not used to show ads, and it does not show whether a payment was received. These cookies are set only after you consent. You may withdraw consent at any time in Cookie settings.

Advertising cookies are used only after you give consent in our cookie banner (or later in Cookie settings). You may withdraw consent at any time. You can also opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings: https://www.google.com/settings/ads Google explains how it uses data from partner sites here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Further details are in our Cookie Policy.

4. Purpose of Processing and Legal Basis

We process personal data to provide and secure the Service, authenticate users via Google Sign-In, sync your learning materials and progress across devices where applicable, respond to support requests, and — where you consent — display advertisements that help keep the Service free. Depending on the processing activity, the legal basis under the GDPR may include: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), our legitimate interests in operating and improving a secure learning Service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and your consent where required for non-essential cookies and advertising (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. No Marketing Use of Email Addresses

We will not use your email address for marketing purposes. We do not send newsletters, promotional campaigns, or advertising emails. Your email address, if received through Google Sign-In, is used only as needed to operate your account and to communicate with you about the Service when you contact us or when such communication is necessary to provide the Service. Advertisements, if shown, appear inside the Service itself and are not sent to your inbox.

We use Google for (a) authentication via Google Sign-In, (b) advertising via Google AdSense, and (c) audience measurement via Google Analytics, only if you consent to analytics cookies. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising at https://www.google.com/settings/ads or via https://www.aboutads.info. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites MicaLingo’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, where applicable.
