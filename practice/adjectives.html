Practice German adjectives: endings, declension, and comparative forms like besser and am besten.

German adjective endings look heavy because they encode article + gender + case at once. After der/die/das the adjective often ends in -e or -en (der alte Tisch, die alte Lampe, das alte Haus in Nominativ). After ein/kein or possessives, masculine Nominativ keeps -er (ein alter Tisch) so you can still hear the gender.

You do not need the full poster overnight. Learn Nominativ and Akkusativ with definite articles first, then ein-words, then Dativ. Comparison is more regular than learners fear: klein → kleiner → am kleinsten, with common irregulars gut/besser/am besten and viel/mehr/am meisten. Practise in the adjectives quiz.
