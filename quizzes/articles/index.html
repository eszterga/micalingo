Every German noun has a gender, shown in the singular by der, die, das. Gender is grammatical: das Mädchen is neuter. The useful method is to learn the article glued to the noun (die Wohnung, not Wohnung) and to remember high-value endings (-ung → usually die, -chen → das).

Articles also change with case (der → den/dem/des). If you can pick the dictionary form but fail in a sentence, the gap is cases, not vocabulary. Read How to learn der, die, das and drill the articles quiz.
